## 5. The RTDS timestamp shape (S3) — unix **milliseconds**, both authorities

- Current page, direct-RTDS example: `"timestamp": 1785178800000` (three
  occurrences across the tabs), with the meaning statement, verbatim: "Use
  `payload.timestamp` as the Chainlink observation time; the outer
  `timestamp` is when the publisher submitted the update to RTDS. Direct
  clients must reconnect and resubscribe after a disconnect."
- Frozen report §10.3, verbatim: the update payload is `{symbol, value
  (number), full_accuracy_value (string integer), timestamp (unix ms),
  window_s}` — **unix ms, in as many words**.

Both authorities agree and neither is ambiguous. This is the evidence base for
`WP-100`'s remediation ruling that `payload.timestamp` is read as
**milliseconds with no seconds-vs-milliseconds heuristic** (the SDK's
`value < 1e12 ? value * 1000 : value` heuristic is not documented for this
field and is not applied on the exact path). Caveat carried forward from
`WP-100` D-6/V14: the **outer** envelope `timestamp` is nullable in the
published Python type; absence is documented traffic.

## 6. The `GET /book` OpenAPI (S4, S5) and its two published contradictions (S6)

The frozen report's source index does not contain the OpenAPI document; it is
indexed here (`WP-070` `follow_up` 3; `packages/polymarket-public`'s
`order-book.ts` header records the 2026-08-27 retrieval this re-verifies).

**Requiredness (S4/S5, re-verified):** `OrderBookSummary.required` lists **all
ten** properties — `market`, `asset_id`, `timestamp`, `hash`, `bids`, `asks`,
`min_order_size`, `tick_size`, `neg_risk`, `last_trade_price`.

**Contradiction 1 — bid/ask ordering.** The OpenAPI (S4 and S5, identical
text): `bids` — "List of bid orders (sorted by price **descending**)"; `asks`
— "List of ask orders (sorted by price **ascending**)". The prose page (S6),
verbatim: "Bids are ordered by **ascending** price and asks by **descending**
price, so the best bid and ask are the last entries in their respective
arrays." The two first-party sources state **opposite orders for both sides**,
today, 2026-09-02. Neither is adopted: the shipped adapter posture stands —
the normalizer imposes the domain's order on both sides rather than trusting
either (`packages/polymarket-public/src/normalize/fields.ts`, per the
`order-book.ts` header), so the contradiction cannot reach a consumer.

**Contradiction 2 — requiredness vs the SDK.** The OpenAPI requires
`timestamp` and `last_trade_price`; the official SDK's own client for the same
endpoint declares both `.nullish()` (recorded by `WP-070` at the pinned SDK
commit). The shipped posture stands and is re-affirmed as the recorded
divergence: the adapter follows the SDK **for these two fields only**
(absence maps to ABSENT, never `"0"`), keeps the other eight required, and
records the divergence with its evidence (`WP-070` remediation M1;
`test/contract/polymarket-public/fixtures/clob-book-rest.json` note). This
report adds the dated confirmation that the OpenAPI still says `required` for
both.
