## 3. C-2 re-verified: the conflict persists, and its shape is now on the record

### 3.1 The USDC side (S1), verbatim

> Taker fees are calculated in USDC and vary based on the share price. The fee
> amount in USDC is symmetric around 50% probability — a trade at 30¢ incurs
> the same dollar fee as a trade at 70¢.

> Fees are rounded to 5 decimal places. The smallest fee charged is **0.00001
> USDC**. Anything smaller rounds to zero, so very small trades near the
> extremes may incur no fee at all.

with the formula, verbatim from the page's fenced block:

```text
fee = C × feeRate × p × (1 - p)
```

and the per-category fee tables headed `Taker Fee (USDC)` (the column header
occurs 4 times, once per category tab, alongside 4 "The fee in USDC **peaks at
50%** probability" sentences). Those 12 occurrences decompose as: 1
deposit/withdrawal mention (§3.4), 2 in the symmetry sentence, 4 column
headers, 4 peak sentences, 1 in the precision sentence.

### 3.2 The pUSD side (S2), verbatim

> Taker fees are calculated in pUSD and vary based on the share price. The fee
> amount in pUSD is symmetric around 50% probability — a trade at 30¢ incurs
> the same dollar fee as a trade at 70¢.

> Fees are rounded to 5 decimal places. The smallest fee charged is 0.00001
> pUSD. Anything smaller rounds to zero, so very small trades near the extremes
> may incur no fee at all.

with the formula, verbatim from the page's fenced block:

```text
fee_equivalent = C × feeRate × p × (1 - p)
```

### 3.3 The four ways the two pages are the same document

This is the observation that characterizes C-2's **shape**, and each half is
independently checkable against the digested bodies:

1. **The prose is verbatim-parallel.** §3.1's and §3.2's first quoted
   sentences are identical **except for the token name** — both sentences, not
   just the "symmetric around 50%" clause. The same holds for the
   fee-precision quote: identical modulo the token name and S1's bold markup on
   the amount. **Same magnitude `0.00001`; same 5-decimal rounding rule.**
2. **The arithmetic is the same,** and S2 says so in its own words: "Rebates
   are distributed using the **same formula as taker fees**." The two fenced
   blocks differ only in the left-hand identifier (`fee` vs `fee_equivalent`).
3. **The pUSD page defers to the USDC page for the numbers.** S2, verbatim:
   "For detailed fee tables for each market category, see the
   [Fees](/trading/fees) page." The page that denominates the quantity in pUSD
   sends the reader to the page that denominates *the same tables* in USDC.
4. **Both pages embed the identical chart asset.** S1 and S2 each render
   `<iframe title="Fee Curves" … id="datawrapper-chart-dJ74e"
   src="https://datawrapper.dwcdn.net/dJ74e/" …>` — the same external
   fee-curve visualization, byte-for-byte the same embed line, on the
   USDC-denominated page and the pUSD-denominated page.

The taker-rebate page (S3) denominates the *rebate payout* in pUSD throughout
("rebates are paid every day in pUSD"; "**paid every day at midnight UTC** in
pUSD"; "A minimum accrued rebate of **\$1 pUSD**"; "a one-time bonus in pUSD")
and never mentions USDC.

### 3.4 What no page says

A search of all four bodies for equivalence, conversion, peg, wrapping, or
bridging language (`convert`, `equivalen`, `1:1`, `one-to-one`, `peg`, `wrap`,
`bridge`, `same token/asset/currency`) returns **no assertion relating USDC to
pUSD**. The only matches are S2's `fee_equivalent` / `total_fee_equivalent` —
a scoring weight in the rebate formula, not a statement about tokens — and the
`datawrapper` iframe URLs.

**No page fetched states that USDC and pUSD are the same asset, states a
conversion rate or mechanism between them, or gives a contract address linking
them.** The frozen report §10.2 records the pUSD, Conditional Tokens,
CtfCollateralAdapter, and NegRiskCtfCollateralAdapter Polygon addresses, and
records that buying consumes pUSD and split/merge/redeem move pUSD; **no
address for USDC appears in any of the four pages re-fetched here, and none of
them cites §10.2's addresses.**

One adjacent, **non-fee** use of the name is recorded so it is not later
mistaken for evidence of equivalence. S1, verbatim: "There are also no
Polymarket fees to deposit or withdraw USDC (though intermediaries like
Coinbase or MoonPay may charge their own fees)." That sentence names USDC as
the **deposit/withdrawal** asset, a different referent from both the fee unit
and §10.2's pUSD collateral. **No page fetched relates the deposited USDC to
the pUSD collateral** — the funding path is not documented on any of these
four pages, and this report asserts nothing about it. See §5 item 2.

## 4. Drift: the liquidity-rewards page no longer names a settlement token

The frozen report §11 C-2 states, verbatim, that "the maker-rebates,
taker-rebates, and **liquidity-rewards** pages denominate payouts in **pUSD**
(daily payouts, '\$1 pUSD' minimum)", and frozen §6's summary of the three
programs is carried into ADR-006's Evidence section as "each in pUSD with a \$1
minimum accrual".

**As of this retrieval that is no longer true of the liquidity-rewards page
(S4), which names no token at all** (§2: zero `usd` substring matches). Its
minimum-payout statement is, verbatim:

> The minimum reward payout is **\$1**; amounts below this will not be paid.

— a dollar figure with **no settlement token named**. The page's other
monetary figures (the "**\$1M** in liquidity rewards" allocation block and its
per-market caps) are likewise unit-free, and its payout timing statement is
"Rewards are distributed directly to maker addresses daily at midnight UTC".

Consequences, stated as drift and not silently adopted:

1. **C-2's scope is narrower today than the frozen report describes.** The
   live inconsistency is between `trading/fees.md` (USDC) and the **two rebate
   pages** (pUSD). The liquidity-rewards page is no longer one of its sides.
2. The frozen report is **not edited** (`protected-contracts.md` §2); it keeps
   its 2026-08-24 wording, and on this point current official documentation
   controls (handoff §1.1).
3. **ADR-006's Evidence phrase "each in pUSD" is, as of 2026-09-03, unsupported
   for liquidity rewards.** The ADR's dated 2026-09-03 §7 amendment records
   this as the correction-of-fact note that `docs/adr/README.md` and
   `protected-contracts.md` §4 require, without editing the original
   snapshot text.
4. **A liquidity-reward payout therefore has no source-asserted denomination.**
   This is a live obligation for the fee/reward accounting work, not a
   curiosity: ADR-006 §7 item 3 denominates an entry "in the unit asserted by
   the source it was derived from", and for this program the current source
   asserts none. Inventing `pUSD` there would be inventing venue behavior
   (`AGENTS.md`). The ADR amendment states the fail-closed rule.

Two further volatility notes on S4, recorded because a later reader will meet
them: the page's "Crypto TWAP Rewards" block is scoped "through the month of
August" and its `\$1M` allocation tables may therefore be a lapsed seasonal
promotion, and the scoring methodology (quadratic `S(v,s) = ((v-s)/v)^2 · b`,
10,080 samples per epoch, scaling factor `c = 3.0`, the `[0.10, 0.90]`
single-sided band) is unchanged from frozen §6. This report makes no claim
about whether the August allocation is still funded.
