## summary

1. **ADR-030 Amendment 2** (2026-10-05; Source: `VENUE-4`). Rules 1-4 are the orchestrator's decisions applying documented facts; rule 5 is an interim ruling.
   - **Rule 1:** trading ids are selected by Gamma `version`: `positionIds` for `"v2"`, and the decoded `clobTokenIds` for `"v1"`, even when both are present. Missing, null and unknown versions are refused, and CLOB `v` can only refuse (C-21).
   - **Rule 2:** `acceptedProtocolVersions` is a reviewed series parameter, in both schema copies and both hashes. **A series trades V2 windows only after a review that accepts `"v2"`.**
   - **Rule 3:** Gamma's condition id is the identity. CLOB and Data API reads send the 32-byte right-padded form, and any other width is refused.
   - **Rule 4:** `GET /v2/resolutions` is a journaled public read, on our own client.
   - **Rule 5, the orchestrator's interim ruling for PAPER and BACKTEST, open to the user.** A journaled row may publish `MarketResolved@1` when the market channel's resolution does not arrive. It must be the only row, keyed by the padded id, with `status` `"resolved"` and raw `payouts` of exactly `[1000000,0]` or `[0,1000000]`. Any disagreement, or any other shape, raises `GATEWAY_SERIES_WINDOW_UNRESOLVED` and publishes nothing. Round 1 added where `resolvedAt` comes from, and the rules for polling and non-terminal rows.
2. **ADR-033 Amendment 1, D5 decided: option 1, by the user.** `polymarket-secure` sends `POST /v1/heartbeats` itself, signed with the SDK's public `buildHmacSignature` and its `credentials` and `account` getters.
   - It is a named exception to handoff §9.12, for that route only.
   - Transport rules: a timeout no longer than the controller's `responseTimeoutMs`; one attempt per call, with resends through the §9.13 budget; the 400 body read under either `error_msg` or `error`, and classified by `heartbeat_id`.
   - It lapses at the first stable SDK release with a heartbeat method.
   - It enables nothing now.
3. **ADR-001 §8 item 6: the bounded binary64 rule,** ruled by the user, for Data API v2 sizes read through the SDK. It states its relationship to handoff §6 invariant 1.
4. **ADR-009:** a `/v2/resolutions` row becomes a second evidence source, with payouts compared as fixed integer vectors (§8). U-11 still applies to V1 (§5).
5. **ADR-010 §4:** the per-surface SDK scope, ruled by the user. Handoff §9.12 and F6 are unchanged.
6. **`docs/adr/README.md`:** the rows are updated.
