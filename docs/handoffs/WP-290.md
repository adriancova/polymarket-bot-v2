## summary

1. **The coordinator** (`ReconciliationCoordinator`) runs §9.17 for all eight triggers.
   - Every trigger pauses new submissions at once.
   - It reads open orders, trades, by-id orders, `/v2/positions` and `/v2/approvals` (V3-E15), the on-chain collateral (never the CLOB cache), the ledger projection, and each wallet-operation member.
   - Every read starts after every request it answers was received, and passes a strict door.
   - It resumes only when every required invariant passes.
2. **Class fixes, each at one choke point.** The orchestrator issued them after repeated rounds:
   - **A, the evidence store** (`evidence.ts`, journaled as `EVIDENCE_RECORDED`). Every validated observation from every source is recorded before classification, whatever the run's soundness or the answer's completeness. Its values are monotone, so an absence or regression can only hold the account.
   - **B, one resolution function.** A break resolves only on a positive judgement of its exact subject by a sound, complete run.
   - **C, halt obligations re-derived every run,** each with its own identity.
   - **D, one run-validity latch,** checked before every commit.
   - **The door layer** (round 11). No validated fact is lost between the wire and the store. Unreadable fragments, such as unkeyed legs, unknown stream kinds and missing keys, become journaled obligations.
3. **The break taxonomy and journal** (`packages/ledger/src/reconciliation/`).
   - Every ambiguity class holds until consistent and is never operator-releasable.
   - Unmatched orders, trades and confirmed deltas become UNATTRIBUTED.
   - The journal is append-only and projects `ops.reconciliation_runs` and `ops.reconciliation_breaks`.
4. **Obligations honoured and tested:**
   - **WP-270:** the quiescence attestation on ABSENT; signed identity with an ambiguity refusal; `requestId` echoed.
   - **WP-280:** its requests and normalized events are consumed.
   - **WP-300 and WP-300c, ADR-032:** no read before receipt; members answered by name; `retryReconciliationRequests` called.
   - **V3-E15:** only the `/v2` routes.
5. **The runbook:** `docs/runbooks/reconciliation.md`.

## known_risks
- **WP290-V7-PHANTOM-PERMANENT-HOLD (LOW).**
  - One transient wrong observation, or an unreadable required identity, holds the account for good: it fails closed, and there is no in-system retraction.
  - A re-delivered EVENT_NOT_FULLY_APPLICABLE request after a restart can hold a trade until it is shown terminal.
  - The retraction ADR is needed before any non-PAPER mode.
- **I-12 (LOW).** A lone foreign exact twin can be adopted PRESENT for an unknown attempt whose own order no source observed. No documented read separates them. It is disclosed in the runbook.
- **Liveness costs after a restart,** and a rebind risk if WP-280's manager is rebuilt under a live coordinator. The composition must restart the coordinator, not rebind it.
- **`CAP1-RESIDUALS` R4-FABLE-01:** an unattributed fill is counted twice against collateral, and this becomes reachable once reconciliation can release a halt in a composition.
