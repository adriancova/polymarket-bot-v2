## summary
1. **The formula (F-63).** A new module, `packages/simulation/src/base-units.ts`, holds `counterAmount = floor(makerAssetFill × takerAmount / makerAmount)`, in integer base units with bigint arithmetic (1 share = 1 pUSD = 1,000,000 base units, F-73).
   - Every simulated maker fill uses it: Tier 0 and Tier 1 taker fills on both sides, Tier-0 maker fills, and Tier-1 band fills.
   - Every fill carries `collateralAmount`, the pUSD it moved before fees, and venue cash and replay economics use it.
2. **The collateral target.** A FOK or FAK BUY can target collateral, as the venue documents.
   - The share size is converted once, at the limit price, and floored to base units.
   - The walk spends the budget level by level, so price improvement buys more shares.
   - GTC and GTD BUYs, and every SELL, target shares.
   - **It is OFF by default** (`fokFakBuyTarget: "SHARES_UNDOCUMENTED"`), pinned and documented. So the shipped PAPER trader still sizes FAK and FOK BUYs in shares, which the venue does not.
3. **Fees** are unchanged: they already matched F-64.
4. **New refusals:** a price at or below zero, an action other than BUY or SELL, and a target below one base unit.
5. **Replay goldens:** none changed.
