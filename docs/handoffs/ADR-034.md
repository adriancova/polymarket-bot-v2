## summary
1. **The venue addendum** `verified-2026-10-06.md` holds facts F-79 to F-104, conflicts C-24 and C-25, unknowns U-49 to U-54, and drift rows E-28 and E-29. Its sources are 12 official pages fetched on 2026-10-06, and `@polymarket/client` and `@polymarket/bindings` 0.12.0, checked byte-identical to the published tarballs.
   - **Order types:** FAK and FOK use an unsigned `orderType` with `expiration` "0". The SDK makes them only through `createMarketOrder`, where a BUY is sized in collateral.
   - **Timestamps:** order and trade events carry a millisecond `timestamp`. REST instants are in seconds. Placement and cancel answers carry no instant.
   - **Size precision:** 2 decimals.
2. **ADR-034 has four decisions:**
   - **D1** (`WP340-F1`): venue-time ordering of late order observations. A stale one is recorded and does not halt; a newer one halts. A pair that venue-timed evidence may still order is held, with submissions paused. A pair it can never order halts at once.
   - **D2** (`CO3-N1`): one executable quantity, floored to the 0.01 grid once in the planner.
   - **D3** (`CO3-N2`): time-in-force carried from the plan to the SDK, and refused before signing when unsupported.
   - **D4** (`V2-10B`): collateral-targeted FAK and FOK BUYs, exits sized by the held position, and the residue held to resolution.
3. **The implementation plan** has three rounds, strictly in order: `OMS-QTY` (D2), `OMS-VENUE-TIME` (D1) and `TIF-COLLATERAL` (D3, D4). Each has named paths and acceptance criteria, and each has two reviewers.

## known_risks
- **Unobserved assumptions:** D1 rests on an inference about what `timestamp` names (A F-98) and on assumption A12. A11 and A13 are also unobserved. The first execution probe checks them, and no FAK or FOK runs above PAPER before then (Open items 2 and 3).
- **A band-crossing fill can floor an owner's part to zero.** That refusal fails closed, at a liveness cost.
- **The unrecorded-leg deferral** holds the account, and no runbook step exists for it yet.
- **R3 (`TIF-COLLATERAL`) has a large scope.** It needs the WP-200 and WP-320 owners' consent, and it changes PAPER fills and goldens (D4).
- **LOW R6-L1:** two passages wrongly say only one route leaves a collateral-targeted BUY CANCELED. A cancel answer and a read with m = 0 also do; both fail closed.
- **LOW R6-L2:** D4.7 item 4 should say MATCH_IN_TRANSIT while in transit, and MATCH once CONFIRMED.
