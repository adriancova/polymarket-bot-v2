## summary

1. **The fencing lease** (`FencingLeaseStore`, `ops.fencing_leases`):
   - acquire, renew, record the heartbeat id, release, revoke, and check validity;
   - expiry runs on the database clock;
   - every holder write names the lease, the token and the holder, so a stale holder matches nothing, and migration `0008`'s trigger refuses its submissions;
   - a takeover expires the lapsed incumbent, and the next token is the high-water mark plus one, under WP-040's advisory lock;
   - PAPER, BACKTEST and SHADOW are refused before any SQL.
2. **The heartbeat controller** (ADR-033 D1–D4 and D6), behind `OrderHeartbeatTransport`:
   - `assertSignerGate` runs first;
   - it sends only on `permitted === true`;
   - the id chain allows one 400 recovery; repeated 400s raise `LIVE_FENCING_CONFLICT`;
   - heartbeats are filed as `clob.heartbeat` at `ORDER_HEARTBEAT`;
   - it starts lapsed, and measures the 10 s lapse from send time on the monotonic clock.
3. **Live safety** (`LiveSafety`):
   - **the fencing authority:** a local deadline with margins, where a lost grant latches;
   - **the health lease:** all seven §9.18 inputs, each with a maximum proof age, including OMS progress and the reconciler's own status;
   - **kill-switch enforcement:** a durable, fail-closed read of `ops.kill_switch_events`.
     - Only a GLOBAL or own-ACCOUNT halt or cancel stops the heartbeat.
     - MARKET and STRATEGY_INSTANCE switches cancel without stopping it.
     - Refused releases never take effect.
     - Stranded cancels are released.
   - **eligibility:** geoblock and closed-only (D-24); an ambiguous, failed, stale, blocked or closed-only answer blocks entries;
   - **ADR-033 D6 lapse recovery,** through WP-290's coordinator;
   - **a submission fence** in front of the OMS venue port;
   - **WP-290's halt port,** routed into the gate.

## known_risks
- **A venue cancel path that never answers** leaves its obligation open until the OMS's progress proof fails health, which stops the heartbeat (R5-L1, ADR-033 D1 item 3).
- **A `cancelTimeoutMs` below normal venue latency over-cancels,** which is the safe direction.
- **Kill-switch scope references** that are not canonical are refused for ACCOUNT, or only paged for MARKET and STRATEGY_INSTANCE (R4-L1).
- **The IP-budget split with the data gateway** cannot be solved in these paths (WP-310 follow_up 3).
- **Not wired:** the live composition root binds all of this, after ADR-033 D5.
