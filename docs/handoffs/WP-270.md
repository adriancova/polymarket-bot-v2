## summary

`packages/oms` is a layer-1 package that depends only on `@polymarket-bot/decimal`. It reaches the venue, the store, the payload cipher, inventory and the reconciler only through injected ports. A compile-time test proves that WP-260's `SecureVenueClient`, `SignedOrderEnvelope.fromPersistedPayload` and WP-300's `ReservationService` satisfy those ports.

1. **State machines.**
   - The order, submission-attempt and settlement machines each have an explicit transition table.
   - Order and attempt states match the `0005` database enums exactly.
   - A transition outside a table faults the manager.
   - Unclassifiable evidence goes to SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN for a placement, or RECONCILING for an order.
2. **Signed-payload persistence.**
   - One store transaction writes the attempt row (ciphertext only, the salt and the plan link) and moves the order to SIGNED.
   - The cipher's output is refused if it contains the plaintext or the signature. It is verified by decrypt and restore before anything depends on it.
   - `expectedOrderHash` is null: STOPPED (see follow_up 2).
3. **The submission protocol (§9.11 steps 1–10).**
   - The attempt id is drawn before signing, and a durable SENDING mark is written before every transmission.
   - Every lost or unclassifiable answer becomes SUBMISSION_UNKNOWN: 401, 425, 429, `unmatched`, a timeout, a dropped socket, a throw, a malformed answer, or the watchdog.
   - Reconciliation requests carry the signed identity and an unguessable token (ADR-032). An answer binds only to the attempt's current, delivered request.
   - **The salt gate.** A new salt is allowed only when every earlier attempt in the group is closed: abandoned, or terminal with a confirmed final size. The check and the claim are one synchronous step, and the new order is capped by the group's exact remainder.
   - ABSENT answers need `transmissionQuiescent: true`. A seeded property found a duplicate-order path without it.
   - The same signed order is retransmitted only on the 425 restart path, after an accepted, quiescent ABSENT.
4. **Cancel and replace** go through the salt gate. POST_ONLY and TRADING_UNAVAILABLE gate placements, never cancels.
5. **Attribution and reservations.**
   - Fills are allocated across intents in sequential bands, exactly.
   - Each fill consumes exactly its debit, and only the unused remainder is released, once the final size is confirmed.
   - Fills and observations that race their placement answer are retained and applied, never dropped (round 3).
6. **Recovery.** `OrderManager.open` loads the store first, and starts paused while anything is unresolved.

## deviations
- **18 design decisions** are implemented in their most fail-closed form, and recorded as the orchestrator's interim rulings (2026-10-03). The full list is in `~/pmb-rounds/wp-270/handoff-r0.md` … `handoff-r3.md`. Examples:
  - ABSENT needs a quiescence attestation;
  - a 425-only same-salt resend;
  - release only on a confirmed final size;
  - sequential fill bands;
  - only a late acceptance is adopted;
  - a FAILED settlement halts;
  - venue mode is a caller snapshot.
- **A breaking change to `GroupSpec`** (`groupOrdinal`, `groupKind`, `limitPrice`). No caller exists yet.

## known_risks
- **Before any live mode, ADRs are needed for:**
  - the quiescence horizon: no venue fact bounds it, and WP-290 must honour it;
  - the same-salt resend on the 425 path: the venue does not document deduplication;
  - the expected order hash (see follow_up 2).
- **WP270-R4-01 (LOW).** The venue-id adoption is committed before the drain of retained evidence, so the two are not crash-atomic.
- **OP-R4-01 (LOW).** Three guards of the round-3 fix are unpinned.
- **The at-rest check** refuses no-op and wrapping ciphers, but cannot prove cryptographic strength. The composition's AEAD cipher is the guarantee.
- **WP-190's ids are not UUIDv7,** and the OMS refuses them. The mapping belongs to the composition.
