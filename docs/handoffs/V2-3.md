## summary
It implements ADR-030 Amendment 2, rules 4 and 5, and item 7.

1. **The read.** Each admission cycle, the gateway reads `GET /v2/resolutions?condition=<32-byte form>` for each confirmed window past its scheduled close with nothing owed or published.
   - It is our own client in `packages/polymarket-public`, with no SDK and no credential.
   - The interval is the admission `pollIntervalMs` (default 30 s, floor 5 s).
   - The configuration door refuses a total read rate above 40 per 10 s, which is 5% of the Data API's documented 800.
   - Each read times out after 5 s.
2. **Journal before derive.**
   - Every answer is journaled before it is judged, with its HTTP status in the WAL endpoint's fragment.
   - An answer the WAL refuses derives nothing.
   - `replayJournaledResolutionAnswer` re-derives the finding from the WAL alone.
3. **Publishable.** A row publishes only if all of these hold:
   - it is the only row;
   - its `condition_id` equals the padded id, compared as bytes;
   - its `status` is exactly "resolved";
   - its raw `payouts` is exactly `[1000000,0]` (YES_WIN) or `[0,1000000]` (NO_WIN), by value;
   - its well-formed `resolved_at` becomes `resolvedAt`.

   Such a row publishes `MarketResolved@1`, and the window retires RESOLVED. `["1","0"]` and `[1,0]` are refused.
4. **Pending, failed, refused.**
   - **Pending:** `{"data": []}`, `{"data": null}` (R2-OPUS-L3), or a non-terminal status.
   - **Failed:** no answer, a non-2xx status, or a body that is not JSON.
   - Neither raises anything of its own. The bound's incident carries the latest result.
   - **A refusal** raises `GATEWAY_SERIES_WINDOW_UNRESOLVED` at once, and ends the row path.
5. **Two sources.** The channel is never held back, and the first resolution stands.
   - An agreeing late row is a harmless repeat (R2-OPUS-L1).
   - A disagreement, in either order, publishes nothing from the row, and is raised under its own scope, `<feedId>:<window>:resolution-disagreement`, which outlives retirement.
6. **A refusal survives a restart (R2-OPUS-L2).** An admission-ledger marker, `resolution-row-refused:<window key>`, carries it, with no schema change.
   - **Stated residuals:** a failed marker write means a restart reads the window again, and the marker is pruned two days after the close.
7. **Item 7: not yet admissible.** A `"v2"` window whose `positionIds` is absent or null gets the new verdict `NOT_YET_ADMISSIBLE`.
   - Nothing is recorded or raised, and it is re-judged at each poll.
   - It is refused for good if the ids are still missing at its scheduled open.
   - Ids that arrive in time give a byte-identical admission.
   - V21-FABLE-01 and V21-FABLE-02 are pinned.

## The orchestrator's rulings
Both are interim rulings for PAPER and BACKTEST, recorded in ADR-030; the user may confirm or overrule them.
- **2026-10-06, item 7:** a window with ids not yet available is held until its scheduled open, rather than refused for good.
- **2026-10-06, a narrowing:** item 7 applies to `"v2"` only. Holding `"v1"` would need the series-window door, which reads any non-string `clobTokenIds` as null and would let a wrong-typed V1 window be admitted later. V1 windows have always been listed with their ids, and V1 ends at the switchover. So a `"v1"` window with absent, null or wrong-typed ids is refused at once, as since V2-1.
- **Also open to the user:** Amendment 2 rule 5 itself (a row may publish the resolution), which dates from `V2-0`.
