# Residuals

The open residual items: known risks and owed follow-ups that no round has closed. One row each, with an owner. The brief, [`IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md`](../../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md), does not repeat them.

- **Affects PAPER** holds every row that can matter to a PAPER run, and every row in doubt.
- **Before any live mode** holds a row only when its own text puts it on a live-only path. Examples: a live adapter, the wallet inventory, the authenticated rate limits, or a restore that a PAPER restart never performs.
- A row that names a protected area is under "Affects PAPER" unless its text says it is live-only. The protected areas are ambiguous submissions, duplicate orders, position sizing, reliable exits, persistence ordering, risk and halts.
- A round that opens a residual adds its row. A round that closes one removes its row, in the round's own branch, and names it in its handoff ([`README.md`](README.md) rule 11).
