## summary

`WP-030` records the locked architecture decisions and integration boundaries as
twelve accepted ADRs plus two contract documents. Nothing here is new design: the
decisions are already locked by handoff §2 and §20, and this package writes them
down with their evidence so later packages conform to a record rather than to a
re-reading of the specification.

**What each ADR settles beyond restating the spec** — every one of these is a
place where the handoff referenced something it did not define, or where a prior
work package asked for ratification:

- **ADR-001** approves `decimal.js` (handoff §2.1 says "or an ADR-approved
  exact-decimal equivalent"), ratifies the §7.3 canonical grammar as an explicit
  EBNF, the three-grammar split (canonical boundary / hash input / venue input)
  with the strict no-coercion boundary, the domain-tagged SHA-256 preimage
  `polymarket-bot/decimal/v1:` + canonical, and the precision/rounding table
  (non-rounding add/sub/mul; 34-digit `ROUND_HALF_EVEN` division). It also fixes
  five adapter obligations at the venue edge (empty-string optional decimals,
  `DecimalishSchema` number acceptance, `full_accuracy_value` for TWAP,
  six-decimal integer amounts, and that denomination is not a decimal concern).
- **ADR-002** ratifies the envelope as implemented with `gatewayEpoch`/`ingestSeq`
  **required**, `(gatewayEpoch, ingestSeq)` as the only ordering key, the
  version-per-emitted-field-set-change policy forced by strict unknown-key
  schemas, the `(eventType, schemaVersion)` registry as a total injective key,
  **reserves the top-level payload key `venue`** repository-wide for provenance
  with envelope `source` authoritative, ratifies the `TradingParametersChanged`
  vocabulary (`status` cited to §10.1; `open_time`/`close_time` as parameters, not
  duplicates of `MarketOpened`/`MarketClosing`), states the
  **runtime-must-not-inherit** rule for `WP-000`'s fixture-only narrowings, and
  carries **C-1/U-1** as explicitly provisional.
- **ADR-003** records the Redis Streams transport behind an interface and asserts
  **no venue behavior at all**; it also states that the §9.1 5 ms p99 figure is a
  target and that no benchmark has been run.
- **ADR-004** is the approval §9.1 defers to it: append-only JSONL, exact raw
  payload bytes, header/footer-or-sidecar, rotation, periodic `fsync` as a
  *published data-loss bound*, truncate-only-the-final-record recovery, and
  delete-only-after-verified-upload compaction. It decides that heartbeat/protocol
  frames are recorded (they are the evidence that separates "venue silent" from
  "connection dead") and flags that a future binary feed would need an amendment.
- **ADR-005** fixes purity as a package-boundary property, resolves the tension
  between §9.6's watchdog and §6 invariant 3 (a timeout persists exactly one
  runtime-attributed record), ratifies `StrategyContext` staying out of
  `packages/domain`, and **ratifies the inferred `QuoteLevel`/`BasketLeg` shapes**
  with the rationale that anything further is execution policy already carried on
  the intent.
- **ADR-006** fixes per-asset balancing, the scope semantics (virtual allocation
  is a partition of a real balance, never a parallel balance), UNATTRIBUTED plus
  halt, recognition points keyed to settlement state with compensating reversals,
  and records **C-2 (USDC vs pUSD) as UNRESOLVED** with four binding rules that
  prevent an implementation from resolving it by assumption.
- **ADR-007** makes the §9.11 ten-step protocol binding in order, maps the four
  venue order statuses without optimism (`delayed` is never a fill, `unmatched` is
  not a rejection), requires unknown error codes to be first-class UNKNOWN, treats
  425/cancel-only/post-only as transport conditions rather than rejections,
  derives the GTD 60-second offset consequence, and carries **C-3** and **U-7**.
- **ADR-008** records the PostgreSQL lease plus monotonic fencing token (Redis
  explicitly insufficient) and derives three consequences from verified venue
  mechanics: the rotating heartbeat ID is itself lease state, the health-lease
  evaluation budget is bounded by the 5-second cadence, and venue cancellation is
  **credential-scoped** so credential sharing defeats isolation.
- **ADR-009** ratifies the terminal-outcome subset and the **DISPUTED-is-
  non-terminal** ruling, records the absent dedicated dispute event as a follow-up
  (add `MarketDisputed` under a new schema version rather than loosening
  `MarketResolved`), requires 50/50 *support* while marking the 50/50 and
  clarification mechanics **UNVERIFIED (U-6)**, and binds TWAP specs to windows
  RTDS actually publishes.
- **ADR-010** restates the four PAPER defaults as floors, derives the startup
  deny-list from the venue report's per-name credential table (§16.1 + §16.2) and
  records that `POLYMARKET_BUILDER_CODE` is **public attribution, not a
  credential**, and attests the current state: no signer, no credential, no live
  gate requested.
- **ADR-011** grounds one-live-owner-per-market in shared account-level
  resources (collateral, per-signer token buckets, credential-scoped heartbeat,
  and the fee cost of offsetting quotes) and distinguishes it from ADR-008's
  process-level fencing. Round 1 tightened two rows to the report's actual
  support: bucket contention makes a safety cancel *possibly* delayed or denied
  (the report documents no arbitration), and **venue matching behavior for
  same-account orders is NOT DOCUMENTED** and is recorded as a gap.
- **ADR-012** fixes the three-tier hierarchy and the rule that paper fills are
  never evidence of real fill quality, and enumerates nine venue-grounded things
  Tier 1 must model — including that the three incentive programs differ (maker
  rebates pool-shared and midnight-UTC daily; taker rebates tiered with a daily
  payout; liquidity rewards sampled and midnight-UTC daily) but share the
  property that makes them **uncreditable per simulated fill**, and that depth
  reconstruction inherits the unverified C-1/U-1 status.

`docs/contracts/dependency-direction.md` turns §5.2 into a layer table over all 28
packages and 6 apps — **each package in exactly one layer** — plus an exhaustive
§2.1 table of **permitted same-layer edges** (S0 `domain → decimal`, S1

concrete forbidden edges, an honest statement of what is enforced **today**

three-part specification for the CI check §5.2's "circular package dependencies
fail CI" still requires — including that it must fail closed on an unclassified
package and on an unlisted same-layer edge.

`docs/contracts/protected-contracts.md` records the protected paths and what
"frozen" means, the ratification-precedent mechanism with **all six precedents to
date**, the handoff-record requirement and the precision standard the `WP-020`
review established, the §18.4 merge protocol, a consolidated **open venue-fact
register** (fourteen items with report section, owning package, and owning ADR —
twelve at authoring time, plus the two gaps review round 1 surfaced),
and the fixture-narrowing rule.

5. **"Accepted" is the correct status at authoring time.** These record decisions

6. **Layer assignment of packages** in `dependency-direction.md` is my
   classification derived from handoff §5 and §5.2, not a verbatim spec table —
   §5.2 gives the direction and four forbidden edges but does not enumerate which
   package sits in which layer. It is flagged in the document as the data the CI
   check should consume, so it is reviewable and correctable in one place.

   upward edge is needed because the transport's consumers are the layer-3

   downward — not a reclassification.
8. **Same-layer dependencies are permitted when enumerated** (added in round 1).

## known_risks

1. **These are documentation claims about a mostly unbuilt system.** ADR-003,
   ADR-004, ADR-006, ADR-007, ADR-008, ADR-009, and ADR-012 describe components
   that do not exist yet (`WP-040`+). Nothing mechanically checks that a future
   implementation conforms to its ADR; the only enforcement is review at merge
   (§18.4) and the §21 definition of done.
2. **Citation accuracy is the load-bearing property, and nothing verifies it
   automatically.** `WP-000`'s round-5 review found exactly this failure mode in a
   different artifact: a gate that checks a citation *exists* cannot check that
   the citation *supports the claim next to it*, and the WP-000 record flags that
   the report's own gate still has that limitation. Every handoff and venue-report
   citation in these fifteen documents was written by reading the source, but an
   adversarial review should spot-check them rather than trust them — particularly
   the venue-report section numbers, since the report was amended across five
   remediation rounds and some sections (§7.1, §16, §17) were added late.
   **Round 1 confirmed this risk materialized**: four claims cited a real section
   that did not support the sentence beside it (see
   [review history](#review-history)). They are repaired, but the base rate is now
   measured rather than hypothetical, and the unaudited citations should be treated
   as unproven.
3. **The venue report is a 2026-08-24 snapshot** that must be re-verified at each
   phase start (handoff §1.2). Every venue-grounded statement in these ADRs
   inherits that expiry — notably every rate limit, fee rate, reward parameter,
   geoblock tier, and heartbeat timing figure. The ADRs say so at each use, but a
   reader skimming a Decision section could still take a snapshot figure as
   permanent.
4. **Derived venue consequences are my reasoning, not venue statements.** Three in
   particular deserve reviewer attention because they go one step beyond the cited
   fact: the rotating heartbeat ID being lease state (ADR-008 §4.1), the
   health-lease evaluation budget being bounded by the 5-second cadence
   (ADR-008 §4.2), and the 0.5-per-share arithmetic for a 50/50 outcome
   (ADR-009 §5.3, explicitly conditioned on U-6 remaining unverified). Each is
