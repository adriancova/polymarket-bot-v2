## 2. What a rules version is here, and how to identify one for these markets

**What the repository means by it.** Handoff §6 invariant 9 says "Market rules,
settlement specs, fee schedules, tick sizes, minimum sizes, and delays are
versioned." In code:

- **The table.** `catalog.market_rule_versions` (`db/migrations/0002_catalog.up.sql:122-141`)
  holds one row per **market**: `market_id`, `rules_version` (1, 2, … per
  market), `rules_text`, `rules_hash` (sha256 hex), `source_url`,
  `observed_at`. It enforces `unique (market_id, rules_hash)` and is
  append-only (line 141). `catalog.markets.current_rule_version_id` points at
  the current row (lines 143-146).
- **The spec.** A settlement spec's `rules_version_id` is a foreign key to
  **one** `rule_version_id` (line 296). In the package, `rulesVersionId` is an
  optional UUIDv7 (`packages/settlement/src/spec.ts:1335-1342`).
  `settlementSpecReviewBlockers` refuses a review that names none
  (`SETTLEMENT_RULES_VERSION_REQUIRED`, `spec.ts:1977-1985`).
- **The comparison.** `evaluateMarketReadiness` refuses activation unless the
  spec's `rulesVersionId` equals the market's own `rulesVersionId`
  (`packages/universe/src/eligibility.ts:400-430`). The market's value comes
  from `MarketRulesChanged@1.rulesVersionId`, a non-empty string
  (`packages/domain/src/events/market-lifecycle.ts:115-120`).
- **The writer.** `createCatalogRepository(db).recordRuleVersion` exists
  (`packages/storage-postgres/src/repositories/catalog.ts:218-253`). No
  process calls it. The trader registers markets only
  (`apps/trader/src/register/registration.ts:297`). Nothing produces
  `MarketRulesChanged`.

**How a version is identified for these markets.**

- The venue publishes no rules-version identifier. Gamma's `version` field is
  `"v1"` on every window, before and after the 2026-08-07 change, so it is
  not a rules version.
- The only identity the evidence supports is **the content**: the sha256 of
  the `description` bytes, `485ceb1d…` today. The text changed once under the
  same series and title pattern (§1.3). A check by hash catches such a change;
  a check by series or title does not.
- The proposed rule: `rules_text` is `description` verbatim, and `rules_hash`
  is sha256 over its UTF-8 bytes. The `resolutionSource` is reviewed as its
  own checklist item.

**The structural problem (gap G-2).**

- Each 15-minute window is a separate market. It gets its own
  `catalog.markets` row, and so its own `rule_version_id`, even when the text
  hash is identical.
- A series-level spec can name only one `rule_version_id`, and the universe
  compares identifiers. So a spec reviewed against "the rules version" can
  match **at most one window**.
- Closing this gap needs a decision and a contract or migration change, in
  `packages/settlement`, `packages/universe` or `db/migrations`. None of
  those is in this round's paths. Options include:
  - a series-level rules identity keyed by hash;
  - comparing `rules_hash` rather than `rule_version_id`;
  - making the identifier itself the content hash.

## 3. The reading

### 3.1 The sentence

> This market will resolve to "Up" if **[A]** the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title **is greater than or equal to** **[B]** the price at the beginning of that range.

[A] is the settlement value and [B] the reference ("price to beat").

### 3.2 The candidate readings

- **R1, the whole-range TWAP.** [A] is a time-weighted average of BTC/USD over
  the full 15-minute range, `[open, close]`.
- **R2, the 60-second TWAP at the close.** [A] is the 60-second TWAP stream's
  value at the range's end: the average over `[close − 60 s, close]`. [B] is
  the same stream's value at the range's start, the average over
  `[open − 60 s, open]`.
- **R3, other variants:**
  - R3a: the average of the 60-second stream's values over the range (R1
    computed from the named stream);
  - R3b: R2 for [A], with [B] a spot price;
  - R3c: R2 with a different boundary report (for example the first report
    after the instant).

### 3.4 Answers

1. **Which reading does the evidence support?** **R2.** Every documentation
   source except the literal grammar of one clause supports it (rows 2-8), and
   both observations agree (rows 9-10). R1 rests on row 1 alone and conflicts
   with rows 4-7 and 10. R3b conflicts with row 4. R3c is not excluded,
   because the boundary report is undocumented (U-24).
2. **What is "the price at the beginning of that range"?** The 60-second TWAP
   stream's value at the window's open instant, that is, the average over
   `[open − 60 s, open]`.
   - That the price to beat "come[s] from the applicable TWAP feed" is
     documented (row 4).
   - That it is the value at the open instant follows from the rules' "at the
     beginning of that range", and observation agrees (row 10).
   - It is not a spot price, and not a TWAP over the range.
   - Which Chainlink report is "at" the open (one ending exactly at the open,
     or the one containing it) is not documented by Polymarket.
3. **Which time zone, which boundaries, inclusive or exclusive?**
   - The rules define the range by the title, in ET: America/New_York, UTC−4
     until 2026-11-01 and UTC−5 after.
   - On 33 of 33 windows, the title range equals `[eventStartTime, endDate]`
     in UTC, 900 s long (F-15).
   - The settlement value is read at `endDate`, and the reference at
     `eventStartTime`.
   - Each averaging window is the 60 s ending at its instant (row 8).
   - **Inclusivity is undocumented**, for both the instants and the 60 s
     windows. Chainlink writes its window as `12:04:01 → 12:05:01` with no
     open or closed notation. When no report ends exactly at an instant,
     Chainlink's rule is that the containing report applies, with its price
     "observed one second later". Whether Polymarket follows that rule is
     U-24.
4. **Is the comparison GTE?** **Yes.** The rules say "greater than or equal
   to", so a tie resolves **Up**. The old seed's `GT` was wrong: it would have
   settled a tie as Down. Observation cannot tell GT from GTE, because no tie
   occurred in 21 windows (F-28).
5. **Is the ambiguity resolvable from documentation alone?** **Not fully.**
   - Documentation settles the observation type and the window: a 60-second
     Chainlink TWAP read at the open and at the close (rows 4-8). It does so,
     though, through the changelog and the channel reference, while
     Polymarket's own resolution page says the **rules** govern (row 11), and
     the rules sentence reads as R1. So C-15 is a conflict between official
     texts, and only Polymarket can close it.
   - Documentation does not settle: the boundary report and inclusivity
     (U-24); the comparison's precision (U-25); what happens when the stream
     has no report (U-26); and whether, and for how long, an automatic
     resolution can be disputed (U-27).

### 3.5 Recommendation, and the question to ask Polymarket

**The recommended reading is R2**, as the corrected seed states it (§4). It is
the reading the documentation and both observations support. The open points
are each written into the seed as prose that fails closed, and each is a line
on the checklist.

The documented channel for a clarification request is the Polymarket Discord
`#market-review` channel (S-D33 lines 108-111). The draft question:

> For the "Bitcoin Up or Down" 15-minute markets (series `btc-up-or-down-15m`), the rules say: "This market will resolve to "Up" if the time-weighted average price (TWAP) of Bitcoin, generated by Chainlink, of the time range specified in the title is greater than or equal to the price at the beginning of that range," with the resolution source https://data.chain.link/streams/btc-usd-twap-60s-streams. ### 4.3 What the current vocabulary can and cannot express

**R2 is expressible: no STOP.** `ReferenceOpenUpDownModel` on a `TWAP`
observation requires `comparison`, `referenceOpenSource` and the three window
fields, and forbids `strikeSource`
(`packages/settlement/src/models/compatibility.ts`).

Its observation carries `referenceOpen` and `referenceOpenAt`, an
`observedValue` and `observedAt`, and a window whose span must equal
`windowSeconds` exactly (`packages/settlement/src/models/registry.ts:160-243`
and `:376-464`; `packages/settlement/src/observation.ts:77-92`). A 60 s window
ending at the close fits. So would Chainlink's shifted window when no report
ends exactly at the close, because it is still 60 s.

Two limits, for the owners:

1. **The reference open's nature is prose only.** The model accepts any
   decimal as `referenceOpen`. Nothing structural says it must be a 60 s TWAP
   value of the same stream. A producer of observations has to honor
   `referenceOpenSource`.
2. **The boundary-report rule is prose only.** It lives in `windowEndRule` and
   `timestampBoundary`. No field encodes "the containing report".

R1 would also be expressible (`windowSeconds: 900`), so the vocabulary does not
force the choice.

## 5. The sign-off path

### 5.1 What the code does, step by step

1. **A person reviews.** The checklist is the form. **No code records the
   decision** (G-1).
2. **The rules version is recorded.**
   - `catalog.market_rule_versions` holds one row per market (§2).
   - The writer `recordRuleVersion` exists but has no caller (G-3).
   - A series-level spec cannot name one row for every window (G-2).
3. **The spec review is recorded.**
   - In the package, a review is the `verification` variant
     `{status: "VERIFIED", verifiedBy, verifiedAt}`: a non-empty string and an
     ISO instant (`packages/settlement/src/spec.ts:1308-1317`).
   - In the database, it is `catalog.settlement_specs.verification_status =
     'VERIFIED'` with `verified_by` and `verified_at`
     (`0002_catalog.up.sql:312-314`). The check
     `settlement_specs_verification_complete` makes `VERIFIED` and both
     reviewer columns go together (lines 331-334).
   - Only the review columns may change after insert: the trigger
     `settlement_specs_immutable_semantics` (lines 346-353) freezes the
     semantics. See G-10 for what it misses.
   - **The seed file must never carry the review.**
     `packages/settlement/src/seeds.test.ts:58` refuses any seed whose text
     contains `verifiedBy`, `verified_by`, `verifiedAt`, `verified_at` or
     `"VERIFIED"`.
   - **No writer, loader or command** inserts or updates
     `catalog.settlement_specs`. `packages/storage-postgres` has the table type
     (`src/schema/catalog.ts:177-199`) and no repository function. `apps/ops-cli`
     has only `verify-venue`. `apps/control-api` has no settlement route (G-1).
4. **The series is bound.**
   - `catalog.series.binding_approved`, `binding_approved_by` and
     `binding_approved_at` must go together (`0002_catalog.up.sql:34-43`).
     `active_settlement_spec_id` names the bound spec (lines 37, 355-358).
   - The series seed must keep `binding.approved: false` and no
     `activeSettlementSpecId` (`packages/universe/src/seeds.test.ts:55`;
     `packages/settlement/src/seeds.test.ts:98`).
   - The universe requires an `APPROVED` market binding, an approved and
     active series, and a verdict whose spec id is the series' active spec
     (`packages/universe/src/eligibility.ts:201-247`, `:291-397`).
   - Nothing writes the approval. Trader market registration passes no
     `seriesId` (`apps/trader/src/register/registration.ts:297-312`) (G-7).
5. **The activation verdict.** `classifySettlementActivation`
   (`packages/settlement/src/activation.ts:150-229`) returns, in order:
   - `SPEC_MISSING`;
   - `SPEC_INVALID`;
   - `SPEC_NO_PAYOFF_MODEL`;
   - `SPEC_REJECTED`;
   - `SPEC_UNVERIFIED`, for any status other than `VERIFIED`;
   - `SPEC_VERIFICATION_UNSOUND`, when `settlementSpecReviewBlockers` finds a
     problem: no rules version; a model mismatch; or a window missing from the
     caller's `publishedWindowSeconds`, or no list given
     (`spec.ts:1935-2013`);
   - `REVIEWED_MODEL_BACKED` otherwise.

   The **published-window check** (ADR-009 §6 rule 1) takes the list from the
   caller. The only dated value in code is
   `RTDS_TWAP_WINDOW_SECONDS_VERIFIED_2026_08_24 = [30, 60]`, which names the
   deprecated RTDS feed (`spec.ts:1952-1954`). The feed the rules name
   publishes **60 only** (F-25, F-26). Nothing supplies a current list, and
   ADR-009 §6 still names "the RTDS TWAP feed" (G-4).

   (`packages/universe/src/eligibility.ts:89`) correlates the verdict with the

   `modelDependentActivationAllowed`. **No composition root calls it, or

7. **The trader.** §9.8 check 6 refuses an entry

   (`packages/risk/src/policy.ts:45`).

     `markets[].settlementReadiness.modelDependentActivationAllowed`
     (`packages/trading-core/src/config.ts:286-307`; passed through at
     `packages/trading-core/src/loop.ts:2564-2565`).

     example sets it to `false`

### 5.2 What a review unblocks, and what makes the veto pass in PAPER today

**Today, two things lift `RISK_SETTLEMENT_UNVERIFIED` in PAPER. Neither reads
a review:**

1. an operator sets `markets[].settlementReadiness.modelDependentActivationAllowed:
   true` in the trader configuration;
2. or the risk policy sets `requireVerifiedSettlementForEntries: false`, which
   switches check 6 off entirely.

The first is an operator's assertion that a review happened. The repository
documents it as such: "changing it is an operator's assertion about a review
that has happened" (`infra/compose/trader/README.md:153`). Signing the
checklist does not change any of this by itself.

Once the gaps below are closed, a recorded review would unblock this chain:

- the spec verdict `REVIEWED_MODEL_BACKED`, given a rules version and a
  published-window list containing 60;
- then universe readiness `modelDependentActivationAllowed: true`, given an
  approved binding and matching identities;
- then §9.8 check 6 passes, so PAPER entries in `btc-15m-updown` windows can
  reach the remaining checks and, if those pass, fills.

At resolution, the trader takes the outcome from the venue's `MarketResolved`
event (`packages/trading-core/src/event-door.ts:79`;
`packages/trading-core/src/loop.ts:1931-1935`), not from a computed TWAP
(§6).

## 6. The V3-C13 coupling: observing the settlement value in PAPER

**Context.**

- The PAPER trader takes a market's outcome from the venue's own event
  (`MarketResolved@1`, from the market channel's `market_resolved`; §5.2). No
  production code calls `evaluateSettlement`.
- The settlement **value** therefore matters for three things, not for PnL:
  1. an independent check of the venue's resolution and its replay (ADR-009,
     the spec's observation);
  2. strategy inputs, such as the distance to the price to beat;
  3. the published-window check's input.
- The documented live source of the value is PolyBolt `price.crypto.twap`,
  which needs CLOB API credentials (C-13).
- The legacy public RTDS topics are deprecated, with removal "planned one month
  after the `0.11.0` release": about 2026-10-23, by VENUE-3's arithmetic and
  not a venue statement (U-20).

| Option | What it is | Implications |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **O1** Legacy RTDS `crypto_prices_twap_sixty` | Public, unauthenticated; the repository's RTDS adapter already handles a 60 s window (`RTDS_TWAP_WINDOWS = [30, 60]`) | Lasts only until the removal, then nothing. Legacy wire (E18 fixed point). "No snapshot, history, or replay after a disconnect" (the frozen report §10.3). That it is the same Chainlink series as `price.crypto.twap` is not stated. No credential |
| **O2** PolyBolt `price.crypto.twap` | The documented "used by crypto up/down market resolution" series: 60 s, exact decimal `full_accuracy_value`, a two-minute snapshot on subscribe | Needs **CLOB API credentials**, which the PAPER rules forbid (AGENTS.md; handoff §0.2). It would need a user ruling and an ADR: the secure-boundary rule (§9.12), and the undocumented "readonly" key (U-19) |
| **O3** Chainlink Data Streams directly | The stream the rules name | Needs a **Chainlink account** (S-C13: "Sign up … to get started"). That is a credential too, so it needs the same kind of ruling. The public stream page cannot be read without JavaScript (F-27) |
| **O4** Do not observe the value; use the venue's outcome | The `market_resolved` push (documented); Gamma `closed` and `outcomePrices`; CLOB `tokens[].winner` (documented schema) | Public, no credential, and **what the trader does today**. No independent check of the venue's settlement, and no reference value for strategy inputs. Activation still needs a reviewed spec (check 6) |
| **O5** Record Gamma `eventMetadata` | `priceToBeat` once a window opens, and `finalPrice` after it resolves | Public, but **undocumented** and binary floats (U-32). May change without notice. Never a settlement value. Informational only |
| **O6** A proxy from the trader's reference venue (Binance today) | A 60 s average of a spot feed the trader already has | Strategy inputs only. The rules exclude "any other sources or spot markets", and ADR-009 §2 forbids approximating. Carries basis risk against Chainlink's price |

These options are not exclusive. For example, O4 for settlement can sit beside
O5 or O6 for information. Which, if any, to adopt is the user's ruling on
V3-C13. This round decides nothing.
